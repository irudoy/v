# SH7059 VVEL command paths

The VVEL task selects a duration and phase pair by the local mode, applies
corrections and local limits, and converts the result to a shaft command.
The command word and its reverse lookup are internal coordinates.

## 1ME3B

The task at `0x00061D46` calls the mode machine at `0x00061484`. The machine
writes `0xFFFEF50D` at `0x00061502`. The task passes that byte at
`0x000622C4..0x000622C6` to the pair selector at `0x00062472`. The resolver at
`0x00062FAC`, called earlier at `0x00062200`, prepares the candidate pairs.

The task passes the fine speed snapshot at `0xFFFE8198` to the resolver.
The running duration lookup at `0x00063142` uses curve `0x00017276` and
axis `0x00017366`. The startup resolver at `0x0006375E` uses the selected
coolant coordinate `0xFFFE8070` for the normal startup curve `0x000163E4`
and the current coolant coordinate `0xFFFE8073` for the blend curve
`0x00017136`. The throttled helper at `0x00063534` uses the selected
coordinate for the delay curve `0x0001457A`.

The task inverts the primary transfer curve using values `0x00015A56` and
axis `0x00015A76` at `0x000623DA`. After the local bounds it stores the
command at `0xFFFEB91A` through `0x0006243E`. The second call at `0x00062444`
reverses the arrays and writes the reverse coordinate at `0xFFFEA8C0`
through `0x0006244C`.

## 1EK7A

The task at `0x0006303A` calls the mode transition and reset functions at
`0x00062778` and `0x00062FC8`. The working mode at `0xFFFEF455` is passed
to the pair selector at `0x00063778` through the call at `0x000635C0`.
The resolver at `0x000642BE` prepares the fifteen duration and phase pairs
before the selection. The task passes the selected coolant coordinate
`0xFFFE8094` to that resolver. The startup helper at `0x00064A72` selects
curve `0x00016B6C` over axis `0x000182CE` and the throttled helper at
`0x00064848` indexes the delay curve `0x000149E2` over axis `0x000149EA`.

The task uses values `0x00016196` and axis `0x000161B6` in the inverse
lookup at `0x000636E0`, then bounds and stores the command at `0xFFFEBCA6`
through `0x00063744`. The lookup at `0x0006374A` reverses the transfer arrays
and stores the resulting coordinate at `0xFFFEAB98` through `0x00063752`.

The ROM-local lookup arguments establish the directions of the two transfers.
Physical bank assignment, actuator transport and conversion to physical shaft
angle remain open. The coordinate reviews provide the scale evidence.
