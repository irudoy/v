# SH7059 sensor acquisition

The stock 1ME3B and 1EK7A images use the same application vector table base
and timer registers for crank and intake cam position capture. Both images
read two banks of A/D results and use the high three bits of Port F to select
the external analog multiplexer.

## Timer capture

The application startup loads VBR with `0x00020004` at `0x00022360` on
1ME3B and `0x000223F4` on 1EK7A. The stock table and handler bodies identify
the following paths. The register and vector assignments follow the
[Renesas hardware manual](../../../refs/renesas-sh7059-sh7058s-hardware-manual.md),
table 7.4 and section 11.2.

| Vector | Table slot | Capture register | 1ME3B entry / read | 1EK7A entry / read |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 84 `ICI0A` | `0x00020154` | `ICR0A` at `0xFFFFF434` | `0x0002F730` / `0x0002F7E6` | `0x00030454` / `0x0003050A` |
| 100 `IMI1E` | `0x00020194` | `GR1E` at `0xFFFFF44C` | `0x00030B90` / `0x00030BF0` | `0x000318B8` / `0x00031918` |
| 101 `IMI1F` | `0x00020198` | `GR1F` at `0xFFFFF44E` | `0x00030EAC` / `0x00030F0C` | `0x00031BD4` / `0x00031C34` |

The matching crank instruction windows clear the channel 0A capture flag and
store the 32-bit capture into RAM. The matching cam windows clear `IMF1E` or
`IMF1F` in `TSR1A` and copy the captured 16-bit word to RAM when the
corresponding status bit permits it. Physical bank assignment on the SH7059
images is unresolved. The 1EK7A L0 binds the three handlers to their stock
vector pointers and exact capture accesses.

## Analog acquisition

| Function | 1ME3B | 1EK7A |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sample A/D result banks and advance the selector | `0x0002E614` | `0x0002F31C` |
| Select a position and serve A/D results directly | `0x0002EE12` | `0x0002FB2E` |

Both functions use `ADDR0`, `ADDR1`, `ADDR12`, and `ADDR13` at `0xFFFFF800`,
`0xFFFFF802`, `0xFFFFF820`, and `0xFFFFF822`. They preserve the lower 13
bits of `PFDR` at `0xFFFFF74E` when replacing the selector or set one of
the selector bits as part of the scan. Individual connector terminals,
channel-to-sensor assignments, and their downstream value producers require
separate evidence on each image.
