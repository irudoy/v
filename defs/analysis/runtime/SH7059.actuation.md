# SH7059 stock actuation

The stock 1ME3B and 1EK7A ROMs program the injector start compare through
the event scheduler. The throttle, CVTC, and ignition paths below bind
software commands to peripheral writes where the target ROM establishes
the connection.

## Injector start compare

The sequential injector task chooses the channel mask and on the
calculated-delay path passes `4 * uint8(snapshot)` in R4. The other
reviewed tail call supplies literal `0x34`. The compare programmer
writes the 16-bit GR1A register at `0xFFFFF444`. It then clears the
compare flag in TSR1A at `0xFFFFF45E` and enables its interrupt in
TIER1A at `0xFFFFF462`. Sections 11.2.20 and 11.3 of the joint Renesas
SH7059/SH7058S manual and its register list identify GR1A and the
output-compare role. `defs/analysis/fuel/JK08A.fuel.md`, Injection timing
and Homologous targets, describes the canonical mechanism and the
independently reviewed 1ME3B counterparts.

The chain `injection_start_compare_sh7059/chain` binds
`injection_start_delay_snapshot_sh7059/ram` to

compare-programmer meanings. It covers delayed start. Pulse width,
compare-match handling and the final driver pin assignment remain
outside this chain.

## 1ME3B electronic throttle and intake CVTC

The throttle PWM writer at `0x0004F0A4` loads `0xFFFEAD30` at
`0x0004F1EE` and `0xFFFEAD32` at `0x0004F226`. The unsigned
words each pass through the multiply-by-320, reciprocal-multiply
and 320-count cap. The direction masks select the member of each
complementary pair that receives the count. Representative word stores
are `0x0004F27C` to BFR6A at `0xFFFFF510`, `0x0004F2A0` to
BFR6B at `0xFFFFF512`, `0x0004F2B6` to BFR6C at `0xFFFFF514`,
and `0x0004F2BC` to BFR6D at `0xFFFFF516`. The writer also contains
zero-output and fixed fallback branches. The direction masks identify
code channels 0 and 1. Their ECM terminal assignments remain unresolved.

The intake CVTC PWM writer at `0x0011C3A6` selects full-resolution
words `0xFFFE9692/0xFFFE9694` under stock scheme bit 2 at `0x8576`.
Loads `0x0011C41E/0x0011C49C` reach BFR7B/BFR7A at
`0xFFFFF592/0xFFFFF590` through stores `0x0011C482/0x0011C4D6`.
The alternate byte sources `0xFFFE938F/0xFFFE9390` load at
`0x0011C418/0x0011C494`. Under the ordinary mode gates and factor
`K`, the full path uses `floor(w*K*125/8192)` and the alternate
uses `floor(x*K*39/80)`. The 1ME3B cycle programmer at `0x0002C4AA`
sets the paired period. The retained code contract is
`defs/analysis/valve-timing/JK78C.cvtc.md`.

## Ignition compare

On 1ME3B the event task at `0x00078F14` publishes
`apc_output/ram` at `0xFFFE81C3` through `0x0007911C/0x0007912A`
and zero-extends the selected byte in the delay slot `0x00079130`.
The call at `0x0007912E` reaches `0x000311B2`. The normal scheduler
can write GR11A at `0x000313BE` and its immediate reset branch can
write GR11B at `0x00031344`. GR11A and GR11B are 16-bit registers at
`0xFFFFF5C2/0xFFFFF5C4`. POPCR at `0xFFFFF700` selects which
pulse output acts on either compare.

The 1EK7A engine-event table at `0x000223B8` selects the task at
`0x0007A520` through its entry at `0x000223C8`. The task stores the
selected APC byte at `0x0007A816` or `0x0007A824` to `0xFFFE81E7`.
The matching branch holds the same value in R7. Instructions
`0x0007A826..0x0007A82A` load the `0x00031EDA` pointer, call
the scheduler and zero-extend R7 into R4 in the delay slot. The scheduler
writes GR11A at `0x000320E6`, GR11B at `0x0003206C`, and POPCR at
`0x00032110`. The stock task bytes bind the APC handoff. The L0
inventory carries the scheduler entry.

## 1EK7A electronic throttle and intake CVTC

The stock schedule at `0x00021FD0` selects throttle task `0x00050294`.
Loads `0x000503DE` and `0x00050416` read `0xFFFE9694` and
`0xFFFE9696`. The conversion caps the count at 320 and selects the
polarity within each complementary BFR6 pair. The task writes BFR6A,
BFR6B, BFR6C and BFR6D from sites `0x0005046C`, `0x00050490`,
`0x000504A6` and `0x000504AC`.

The stock schedule at `0x00021FB0` selects intake CVTC PWM task
`0x0013AF80`. The full-resolution input loads at `0x0013AFFC` and
`0x0013B07A` read `0xFFFE96B8` and `0xFFFE96BA`. The
corresponding branches write BFR7B at `0x0013B060` and BFR7A at
`0x0013B0DA`.

## VVEL transmission

On 1ME3B, the scheduler copies `0x8C` bytes from `0xFFFEB8BC`
to `0xFFFEB830` at call `0x0002182A` to `0x00022594`.
The registers are prepared at `0x00021778/0x0002177C`,
`0x000217CA`, `0x0002181E` and `0x00021824..0x0002182C`.
The source word `0xFFFEB91A` is at offset `0x5E`, so the copy
lands at `0xFFFEB88E`. `0x00124764` reads that word at
`0x00124804`. The transmit record at `0x985C` holds
`03 08 45 60`: record type 3, length 8, and packed standard identifier
`0x4560 >> 5 = 0x22B`. Compare `0x001248A0` selects the record,
and `0x001248B4/0x001248B6` write the high/low bytes to software
payload positions `0xFFFEB259/0xFFFEB25A`.

The HCAN0 transmit dispatcher starts at record zero and calls
`0x0012041C` for type 3 through `0x001205C2/0x001205C4`. The mover
loads staged bytes at `0x00120548`. Its address helper
`0x0011EE00` computes `0xFFFFD128 + 0x20*record + byte` and its
store helper `0x0011EDEE` executes `MOV.B R5,@R4`. Record zero
therefore writes `0xFFFFD128/0xFFFFD129`, HCAN0 physical mailbox 1
data bytes 0 and 1. The dispatcher submits the accumulated request at
`0x001205F6/0x00120600` to `0xFFFFD020/0xFFFFD022`. The
hardware path is static and conditional on the stock controller and
pending-record gates.

On 1EK7A, `0x00021868` calls the local copy helper `0x00022628`
for `0x94` bytes from `0xFFFEBC40` to `0xFFFEBBAC`.
The length derives from `0x000217DE/0x000217E4/0x000217F4`
and the destination from `0x000217EC/0x00021860`. The VVEL
command word at offset `0x66` is copied to `0xFFFEBC12`.
The separate function `0x00107112` also mirrors the original command
at `0x00107252..0x00107256` into `0xFFFE9F9C`.
The periodic table at `0x0002211C` points to the frame builder at
`0x00144E20`. Its load at `0x00144E6A` reads the latched word.
The record at `0x000099D4` contains `03 08 45 60` for standard
identifier `0x22B`. Writes at `0x00144F72` and `0x00144F74`
place the byte pair at `0xFFFEB58F/0xFFFEB590`. The mover at
`0x00140A80` loads staged bytes and uses `0x0013F45C` for the
mailbox address and `0x0013F44A` for the byte store. For record zero
the destination is HCAN0 mailbox 1 at `0xFFFFD128/0xFFFFD129`. The
builder-to-payload relation cites the stock bytes, and the L0 binds the
mover and helpers.
