ECM terminal abbreviations and wire colors are transcribed from the supplied one-page pinout; expanded functions follow the 2007 G35 Sedan service manual. ECM colors indicate the expected MCU-side function class, not voltage or current rating. Wide-terminal markers follow the connector drawing and do not assert a current rating. Every preloaded MCU relationship and topology is a hypothesis, not an internal schematic fact. Board continuity, component marking, firmware port configuration, and bench behavior remain independent verification steps.
