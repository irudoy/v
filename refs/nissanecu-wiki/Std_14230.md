# ISO-14230

Also known as "KWP 2000". At a lower level, almost identical to the older ISO-9141 protocol. The standard is in multiple parts : 

  1. ISO14230-1 basics, electrical + phys
  2. ISO14230-2 Message formatting, bus initialization
  3. ISO14230-3 Definition of standard Service ID (SID)s.
  4. ISO14230-4 Application to emissions stuff



The most relevant for our needs are parts 2 and 3.
