# ROM family and support matrix

This matrix separates binary homology, editor-definition coverage, production
runtime coverage, executable verification, and hardware evidence. A shared
engine name or similar graph structure does not permit address inheritance.

## Family baseline

| Family | ROMs | MCU and flash | Binary relationship | Address policy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `7MFKAVN86` | JK00A, JK03A, JK03B, JK08A | SH7058, 1 MiB | Exact executable ranges; differences are calibration, identity, diagnostic, and checksum storage below `0x020000` | Same-address executable and semantic inheritance after exact-range validation |
| `8MFK` | JK00C, JK00D | SH7058, 1 MiB | Near-code lineage with insertions, deletions, and relocated storage | Independent address profiles and hook validation for each image |
| JK78C | JK78C | SH7058, 1 MiB | Distinct VQ37 code base | Independent layout, checksum model, allocation, and payload lock |
| `1ND` | 1ND0A, 1ND3B | SH7059, 1.5 MiB | Exact executable ranges within the pair; cross-generation homolog of JK78C | One shared executable layout with independent stock identities and stage hashes |
| `1ME3B` | 1ME3B | SH7059, 1.5 MiB | Distinct VQ37 code base with a fused interpolation library; shares the blocks below `0x008200` and a relocated diagnostic descriptor region with `1ND` | Independent recovery for every address through reviewed `1ND0A` structural correspondence; nothing is inherited from `1ND` or JK78C |

## Diagnostic layer

| Family | DTC codes | Developer Immobilizer | Native RomRaider checksum |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| `7MFKAVN86` | 48 | `0x8725` | `alt` and `alt2` |
| JK00C | 48 | `0x873F` | `alt` and `alt2` |
| JK00D | 48 | `0x8740` | `alt` and `alt2` |
| JK78C | 39 | `0x861C` | `alt` and `alt2` |
| `1ND` | 39 | `0x8668` | `alt2` |
| `1ME3B` | 38 | `0x8671` | `alt2` |

`tools/mapkit/dtc_projection.py` prints these counts per target.

The Cruise Control Switch sets the cruise control configuration byte, whose bit
0 enables the steering switch decoder that Map Switching and Rolling Launch
read. `../patches/cruise-control-switch.md` owns the configuration byte of each
image, the decoders, and the hardware checks.

Each image stores its diagnostic descriptors as twelve-byte records followed
immediately by one 32-bit attribute pointer per record. The pointer table is the
ECU's own attribute reference and reproduces every canonical JK08A attribute
address exactly, so a relocated binding is read out of the target image. A code
is carried only when exactly one target record is byte-identical to the
canonical record. VQ37 declares no P1422 record and no exhaust valve timing
records, which is why its registries hold 39 codes rather than 48, and `1ME3B`
declares no P0443 record either, which is why it holds 38.

The descriptor table occupies a different span in every family:

| Family | First record | End of table | Records |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `7MFKAVN86` | `0x19360` | `0x1A14C` | 297 |
| JK00C | `0x1C4E0` | `0x1D2B4` | 295 |
| JK00D | `0x1C500` | `0x1D2D4` | 295 |
| JK78C | `0x1B7AC` | `0x1C5EC` | 304 |
| `1ND` | `0x1BE00` | `0x1CD84` | 331 |

The `1ND` layout declares one runtime domain, so it projects to a single `alt2`
handler. Its boot and vector SUM16 domains end at `0x8200`, below the lowest
published entity at `0x8524`, so no editor session can leave them stale.

`1ME3B` holds this diagnostic registry beside its own structural export, its
interpolation registry, and its definition projection. Its 334
descriptor records start at `0x1BF58` and its attribute pointers at `0x1CF00`,
against 331 records at `0x1BE00` for `1ND0A`. A 1,936-byte window of that region
is byte-identical to `1ND0A` at a constant `+0x164` relocation, but the run as a
whole is not: the 38 codes the two images share sit at relocations of `+0x14C`,
`+0x158`, and `+0x164` and at index offsets of `-1`, `0`, and `+1`. Byte
identity of the record is what carries the binding, so the projection needs no
address model. The image carries the same VQ37 group `0x37` boundary. Its
Developer Immobilizer flag is the configuration byte at `0x8671`, which the
eleven target consumers of the `1ND0A` flag `0x8668` read. Its four SUM16 checksum domains
reproduce at the `1ND` field addresses while the application XOR and SUM fields
sit at `0x0096F4` and `0x0096FC`; the verifier functions are located and the
layout is registered as `sh7059_1me3b`, which projects to a single `alt2`
handler. Its calibration access library is registered in
`../defs/1ME3B.interp.csv` as 34 fused leaves and censused to 768 tables and
457 axes at candidate confidence. `../defs/JK08A-1ME3B.definition-addresses.csv`
binds the reviewed `1ND` semantic set to those tables and axes through `1ND0A`
structural correspondence and records the unsupported source maps of 1ND, as
`../defs/README.md` states; the three canonical ignition surfaces
among them have no homolog, because the `1ME3B` ignition coordinator does not
carry the gated stock-inert selector path. `../defs/vq37-native-tables.toml` registers thirty-one further tables
and twenty-four axes at code confidence: the two alternate selector arms, the
six mode-selected ignition surfaces of that coordinator with their two 8x8
axes, and the
VVEL target set of the resolver `0x062FAC`, which carries the JK78C role set
with floor curves and a constant mode 14 pair, the second copies of the
shaft-angle conversion and monitor floors, the hot-intake idle pair of the
state-code dispatcher `0x07448C`, and the two curves that only this image
holds, the throttled ramp delay `0x01457A` and the start-up blend target
`0x017136`. Map Switching and CAN telemetry use the 1ME3B
address profile in `../patches/rom-address-profiles.toml`: every stock address
is recovered in the target image, the runtime occupies `0x13B108..0x13F000` of
the audited erased region `0x139DC4..0x13FF00`, and
`../patches/1me3b-runtime-correspondence.csv` records the evidence of every
binding. Of the 60 telemetry fields, 56 bind to 1ME3B ECU RAM; 39 of those
sources are confirmed against the DID pointer table at `0x22AE4`, whose
indices match `0x22A70` on 1ND0A. The compiler packs flag bytes differently from `1ND`: the
MAIN switch bit of the ASCD flag byte `0xFFFEB985` is bit 1, the native fuel-cut
state byte `0xFFFE948A` keeps its active and armed flags in bits 3 and 2, and the knock
surface byte `0xFFFE945B` keeps its high-detonation state in bit 7, which the
telemetry capture moves into bit 6 of the wire byte. The knock function
`0x071D64` keeps no signed Q8 knock timing state, and no target homolog of the
normalized load snapshot or of the requested-torque and Fuel Compensation Main
column coordinates is traced, so those four fields are declared unavailable.

The VQ37 Map Switching model replaces the canonical 2x2 fuel-cut table with
two scalar caps and two independent eight-point curves. Exact stock function,
coordinator, scheduler pointer, calibration bytes, ROM pointers, and RAM bases
are bound in `../defs/vq37-native-fuel-cut.toml`; the caps and curves hold
big-endian uint16 RPM codes. The coordinator bodies are discontiguous Ghidra
functions, so the stock image hash, the scheduler pointer, the exact BSR site,
and the hash of the contiguous predicate bind the executable chain, and no
contiguous envelope stands for a body. Rolling Launch executes the
stock final-mask coordinator first and adds all six cylinder bits only for the
exact request value `0x003F`, which the engine publishes from the captured cut
until engine speed falls below restore.

## Source-map parity

Runtime capability does not imply complete source-map parity. Every visible
canonical map and required axis either reaches a reviewed target address or is
an established architectural removal. No canonical role is left unlocalised.

RomRaider XML and TunerPro XDF publish the same entities on every ROM. The two
formats use different constructs for one control: RomRaider models a reversible
two-state range as a `Switch` table and TunerPro as an `XDFPATCH` entry, so
parity is asserted over exact ROM ranges and their stock and patched bytes.
TunerPro additionally publishes one group patch per DTC group; RomRaider has no
construct for a switch spanning discontiguous ranges. Every group entry repeats
an individual entry.

VQ37 duplicates two canonical roles into a default and an alternate table.
Neither pair carries an engine-bank identity: both members of each pair are read
with the same index byte and the same interpolator, and a state selector picks
one of them. Canonical `0x0A29A` reaches JK78C `0x09EBA`, 1ND `0x0A1C0`, and
1ME3B `0x0A0F4`; canonical `0x1219E` reaches JK78C `0x137E8`, 1ND `0x13C1A`, and
1ME3B `0x13A26`. The alternates `0x09FBA`, `0x137F0`, `0x0A2C0`, `0x13C22`,
`0x0A1F4`, and `0x13A2E` have no canonical source and are registered in
`../defs/vq37-native-tables.toml` with exact stock values and accessor
literal-pool bindings.

On 1ME3B the timing coordinator `FUN_0007323E`, homologous to 1ND0A `0x6F37A`,
reads the default `0x13A26` and the alternate `0x13A2E` through the single
index byte `0xFFFE8194`. The selector is bit 2 of the latch `0xFFFEB9A4`, which
sets when the progress word `0xFFFEB8DA` reaches `0x8000` (`ROM8[0x854E]` =
`0x80`) and releases below `0x7300` (`ROM8[0x854F]` = `0x0D`, 0.898). The same
threshold byte pair `80 0D` sits at `0x8503` on JK78C and at `0x853A` on
`1ND`.

The stock VQ37 configuration executes the mode-selected half of the ignition
coordinator. JK78C `0x069AB8` picks the 16x16 surface `0x0A10A` or `0x0A20A`
by coolant temperature. 1ND `0x074D98` and 1ME3B `0x0797CC` pick one of two
16x16 and four 8x8 surfaces by the state code derived from the VVEL mode:
`0x0A410`, `0x0A510`, `0x0A610`, `0x0A710`, `0x0A690`, and `0x0A6D0` on 1ND and
`0x0A344`, `0x0A444`, `0x0A544`, `0x0A644`, `0x0A5C4`, and `0x0A604` on 1ME3B.
These surfaces have no canonical source and are registered in
`../defs/vq37-native-tables.toml` with a `7F` timing-field mask: the
state-code branch of the coordinator takes only the low seven bits of the
lookup. Bit 7 survives only through the temperature branch, which stores the
lookup at `0xFFFE86BA` on 1ND and at `0xFFFE8632` on 1ME3B. What it selects
downstream is unresolved, so no role publishes it. The canonical
ignition trio reaches three byte-identical copies in the gated coordinator half
of JK78C and 1ND, which the stock configuration bytes `0x8965` and `0x8A2B`
(`0xBA`, bit 5 set) leave unexecuted, so the trio is unsupported on every VQ37
image. The two Map Switching ignition families of every runtime target are the
executed 16x16 surfaces: `0x0A10A` and `0x0A20A` on JK78C, `0x0A410` and
`0x0A510` on 1ND, `0x0A344` and `0x0A444` on 1ME3B. For JK78C and 1ND the
runtime validator requires bit 5 of the stock configuration byte, equal stock
bytes in the three gated surfaces, and their absence from the family set; the
ignition adapter passes them through. The four 8x8 state-code surfaces of 1ND
and 1ME3B are four further Map Switching families. The 1ND coordinator calls
their 8x8 lookup `0x0285B4` through the single literal `0x074FB0`, which the
runtime redirects to an adapter of its own at `0x135080`; the second literal of
that lookup, `0x075570`, serves only the reference surface `0x0A650` of the
stock-inert path and stays stock. JK78C has no state-code dispatch and carries
no such families.

The 1ME3B coordinator `0x0797CC` has no gated half: bit 3 of the stock
configuration byte `0x8A7D` (`0x1C`) selects its state-code dispatch, which
reads the 16x16 lookup `0x029BA8` through the literals `0x079CAC` and
`0x079E24` and the 8x8 lookup `0x0293D8` through the single literal `0x079994`,
redirected to the adapter at `0x13E080`; the literal `0x079E40` serves only the
reference surface `0x0A584` and stays stock. The image also holds a second copy
of the coordinator at `0x0791A4..0x0797CB` that no pointer or branch of the
occupied image reaches. Its private literals `0x079404`, `0x079420`,
`0x0796F4`, and `0x0799A4` stay stock. The profile validator proves the absence
of any inbound reference, and the runtime validator requires the set of lookup
literals loaded outside that copy to equal the hooked set and every load of a
state-code table base to be followed by a load of the hooked literal.

`FUN_000797CC` is the only reader of `0xA344`, at eight sites, and of `0xA444`,
at six. It addresses them through lookup `0x29BA8` over axes `0x13726` and
`0x13716`, coordinated by the word `0xFFFE819E` and the byte `0xFFFE8034`,
which is replaced by `0xFF` when bit 3 of `0xFFFE945A` and bit 1 of
`ROM8[0x8A7C]` are set. The state code is `0xFFFEB826`: 1 selects `0xA444`, 2
and 8 select `0xA344`, and 3, 4, 5, and 6-7 select the 8x8 surfaces `0xA544`,
`0xA644`, `0xA5C4`, and `0xA604` over axes `0x13DCE` and `0x13DC6` through
lookup `0x293D8`. `FUN_00050110` writes the code through `FUN_00050310` from
the published VVEL mode `0xFFFE99C2`, whose mode byte `0xFFFEF50D` is published
by `FUN_00061D46`. The output `0xFFFE8606` crossfades the low seven bits of the
selected surface against the held value `0xFFFE8607` by the weight `0xFFFEB824`
(`0x8000` is 1.0, `0x40` the midpoint); the held value refreshes on a code
change, and `0xFFFEB82E` carries the previous code. Consumer `FUN_00077680`
stores `max(0, 0xFFFE8606 - 0xFFFE818F)` into `0xFFFE8602`. With bit 3 clear
the coordinator would instead select by the coolant latch `0xFFFE8073` (DID
`0x1101`, entry of DID table `0x22AE8`) against `ROM8[0x8BED]` and
`ROM8[0x8BEE]`, both 120 (70 degC), interpolating between the thresholds
through `FUN_0002238C`; stock never executes that branch. The reference output
`0xFFFE85E4` reads `0xA344` while bit 0 of `ROM8[0x8659]` is clear, and with
that bit and bit 4 of `0xFFFEB979` set it reads the 8x8 surface `0xA584` over
`0x13706` and `0x136FE`.

The mode pair is identical across all four VQ37 images, and its axes are equal.
As `x - 64`, `0xA344` spans -23 to +17 and `0xA444` spans -23 to +12; they
differ by -5 to +33 in 114 of 256 cells, and bit 7 is set in 119 cells of each.
On 1ND0A the mapper `FUN_0004C4B8` is byte-identical to `FUN_00050310`, and
consumer `FUN_000736F4` takes either the trio samples `0xFFFE866C` and
`0xFFFE866D` or the mode output `0xFFFE8690`, by the same configuration bit 5
that gates the trio. 1ME3B runs the same chain without the trio branch and
without that bit test.

The compensation accessors publish four parallel channels. The two 16x16
surfaces share index byte `0xFFFF22F8` and axes `0x13AF8` and `0x13B18` and
write adjacent bytes `0xFFFF1E91` and `0xFFFF1E92`; the consumer at `0x40E86`
selects one channel per state code and crossfades it against the previous
state's channel by a normalized progress weight where `0x8000` is 1.0. Both
bucket cells initialize to 1 at `0x30F4A`, and on a latched code change
(`0xFFFF45A3` bit 4) the consumer substitutes the frozen snapshot `0xFFFF1E93`,
taken from `0xFFFF114C`, for the origin bucket's value. The
eight-point timing accessor `FUN_000644D0` reads index byte `0xFFFF11A2` on both
branches and selects the alternate through bit 0 of `0xFFFF4525`, a latch on the
u16 transition progress at `0xFFFF447E` that sets at `0x8000` and releases at
`0x7300`. Bit 0 of `0xFFFF23AF` gates the whole accessor and must be zero, so
the selector bit means that the transition has completed.

`0xFFFF447E` is the normalized progress of the transition between two states of
the eight-position machine. `0x46804` writes it as `trunc(f * 32768)` clamped to
`[0, 0xFFFF]`, which makes `0x8000` exactly 1.0. `FUN_00046888` integrates the
float behind it at `0xFFFF995C`, clamped to `[0.0, 1.0]`, stepping by the value
at `0xFFFF2824` divided by 32768, with sign `+1` when the state code at
`0xFFFF2808` is 5 or 15 and `-1` otherwise.
`FUN_000469E0` resolves the endpoints per state: state 1 is the constant
`100.0`, and states 2 through 8 read curves `0x1505C`, `0xD94E`, `0xD9CE`,
`0xDA4E`, `0xDACE`, `0xDB4E`, and `0xDBCE` at scale `1/163.84`.

The state code is a status-block byte at `0xFFFFA22F`. `FUN_00054FD0` publishes
it to `0xFFFF2808`, and `FUN_0004692A` reduces it to eight buckets: raw `5` maps
to `2`, `3` to `3`, `10` to `4`, `12` to `5`, `13` to `6`, `14` to `7`, `15` to
`8`, and every other raw value to `1`. `FUN_0004678C` derives the origin and
destination buckets, the progress at `0xFFFF447E`, and the blend weight at
`0xFFFF43F0`. Scheduler `FUN_00020F1A` copies the `0x6C`-byte block at
`0xFFFF4468` to `0xFFFF43FC` and shuffles the current and previous copies, which
places the origin bucket at `0xFFFF4414`, the destination bucket at
`0xFFFF43EE`, and the applied weight at `0xFFFF43EC`. The compensation consumer
maps bucket `2` to the alternate surface, bucket `8` to the separate table
`0x13AE8`, and every other bucket to the default surface.

`FUN_00054E16` snapshots the machine inputs into `0xFFFFA204` onward, taking
`0xFFFFA219` from `0xFFFF1066` and `0xFFFFA21A` from `0xFFFF1069`. Periodic
runner `FUN_00020F1A` copies six bytes from `0xFFFF106A` to `0xFFFF1064` at the
head of every pass, so `0xFFFF1069` is the previous-cycle copy of the coolant
byte that service-0x22 DID `0x1101` binds. `FUN_00054B2C` takes the cold path
while `0xFFFFA21A` is below `ROM8[0x84A6]` or `0xFFFFA219` is below
`ROM8[0x84A4]`, the previous raw state is not `5`, `10`, `12`, or `13`, and, if
the previous raw state is `4`, `0xFFFFA21A` is also below `ROM8[0x84A5]`. That
path enters raw state `4` through `FUN_00054AC4` or raw state `3` through
`FUN_00054AE8`. All three stock JK78C thresholds are `120`, the `70 C` grid
point of coolant axis `0x14208`, which carries `90, 100, 110, 120, 130, 140,
150, 160` for `40 C` through `110 C`.

Above the threshold the machine resolves three booleans that `FUN_00054FD0`
flattens next to the state block. `0xFFFFA311` mirrors bit 7 of `0xFFFF455B`
and routes to raw state `13` when `0xFFFFA30F`, bit 6 of `0xFFFF23C6`, is set
and to raw state `12` when it is clear. With `0xFFFFA311` clear, `0xFFFFA310`,
bit 7 of `0xFFFF456F`, routes to raw state `15`. With both clear,
`FUN_00054A62` enters raw state `10` while `0xFFFFA30E` is set and raw state
`5`, the only bucket-`2` state, while it is clear. Raw states `3`, `4`, `10`,
`12`, and `13` all reach the default surface, so the alternate surface is the
fully qualified terminal state and the default surface covers every other
condition.

The machine carries two pass counters and no absolute-time input.
`FUN_00054FD0` is entry `98` of the same 144-entry periodic list, so the
resolver runs once per background-loop pass. `FUN_000546BA` increments
`0xFFFFA220` with saturation at `0xFFFF`; `FUN_000543EC` clears it in the same
block that writes raw state `2` to `0xFFFFA22F`, and `FUN_00054C5A` clears it
together with `0xFFFFA21E`, `0xFFFFA222`, and `0xFFFFA226`. `FUN_00053C3E`
admits raw state `13` on its second cold path only while `0xFFFFA220` is below
`ROM16[0x9194]`, which is `2000` passes or `20.48 s`. `FUN_00053E6A`
increments `0xFFFFA224` while `0xFFFFA30A` is zero and releases at
`ROM8[0x848C]`, which is `70` passes or `0.717 s`. Those are the only
timer-shaped thresholds among the calibration scalars the machine reads, and
every remaining input is a coolant comparison, one of the three calibrated
comparators with hysteresis, or the `19`-tick window at `ROM8[0x8B06]`. The
vendor cam-map chain recorded in `../refs/vendor/facts.csv` places a
`First Idle` selection about `40 s` after start, which is about `3906` passes
at this cadence; no threshold of that order exists, so that chain does not

`FUN_00090C9C`, `FUN_00090DCA`, `FUN_00090F3A`, `FUN_000911A2`, and
`FUN_000CE068` read. JK08A carries mass airflow at `0xFFFF101C` and
`0xFFFF101E`, which this image publishes from `0xFFFF1024` and `0xFFFF1026`, so
the two RAM layouts do not agree at this pair.

`FUN_00093164` forms floor `0xFFFF1976` from the flow through the current
throttle opening. It doubles the interpolation of throttle angle-to-area
conversion `0x14538` over opening axis `0x167E4` at
`min(0xFFFF1326 * 4, 65535)`, stores that rate at `0xFFFF1284`, and converts it
with the exact formula `FUN_000923CA` applies to `0xFFFF199C`: `0xFFFF1828` is
`0xFFFF1284` times `262144` over `148` times `0xFFFF1710`, using `FUN_00021E0C`
twice and `ROM8[0x8B0D]` as the divisor. It maps that per-revolution value
forward through the same `0x16744` and `0x16904` pair below `45187` and
`0x16784` and `0x16944` pair above it that `FUN_0009248A` uses, and stores the
result at `0xFFFF1976`. It then runs surface `0x0FACE` forward through
two-dimensional interpolator `0x280C8` at the current speed and that floor, and
stores the request that corresponds to it at `0xFFFF19A4`. `FUN_0009006C`
writes `0xFFFF1326`; stock `ROM8[0x8871]` has bit `3` clear, so the written
value is a direct copy of opening coordinate `0xFFFF11FA`. `0x14538` and
`0x167E4` are the same conversion and axis that the substitute path
interpolates at `0xFFFF43D8` and `0xFFFF43DA`.

`FUN_00092A80` records both comparisons as latched bits. Bit `0` of
`0xFFFF23E7` is set while `0xFFFF197E` plus `ROM16[0x8DEA]` reaches
`0xFFFF1746`, and bit `7` of `0xFFFF23E6` is set while `0xFFFF1976` reaches it.
Stock `ROM16[0x8DEA]` is `0`, so the first comparison carries no hysteresis,
and stock `ROM16[0x8D2C]` is `0xFFFF`, which disables the alternate comparison
against `0xFFFF11FA` and its `ROM16[0x8DAA]` offset. `0xFFFF1978` is
`0xFFFF1976` while both bits are set and `min(0xFFFF1746, 0xFFFF197E)`
otherwise, which is the ceiling clamped between the two limits whenever the
floor does not exceed the request. The domain is therefore closed: `0x1552C`
spans zero to full scale, `FUN_00092A80` converts a point in it to a rate
through the angle-to-area constants, and `FUN_000923CA` converts the summed
rate back to the same domain. The percent scale the JK08A catalog assigns
`0x14114` remains a candidate; the normalized domain and the conversion between
it and a rate are exact in this image.

Bank identity is available from the ROM itself where it exists. The service-0x22
DID tables bind per-bank RAM directly, and their ordering agrees with the factory
manual rule that assigns bank 1 to the lower code of every per-bank DTC pair.
The DTC descriptor table does not repeat that order: JK78C group `0x49` stores
P1089 on even sub-indices and P1092 on odd, group `0x37` stores P0021 below
P0011, and group `0x3D` stores P0051 below P0031. Descriptor position carries no
bank convention, so a per-bank claim requires a DID binding or a consumer trace.

The `8MFK` projections carry no unsupported source row. Every visible canonical
map and required axis reaches a reviewed JK00C and JK00D address. The
stationary rev-limiter and accelerator-pedal thresholds are bound through the
shared literal pool of the relocated calculators: JK08A `FUN_0004F324`
corresponds to JK00C `0x050C9A` and JK00D `0x050C96`, and JK08A `FUN_00047A56`
corresponds to JK00C `0x048ADC` and JK00D `0x048AE0`.

### VQ37 removes exhaust camshaft timing control

The diagnostic publication layer of both VQ37 images carries the removal, and
`../defs/JK78C.can.md` records it: the DTC descriptor table holds no exhaust
camshaft monitor and no P1078 or P1084 record, the service-0x22 pointer tables
leave the exhaust identifier triple `0x1164`, `0x1165`, and `0x1166` without a
single reference while the intake identifiers stay live, and five independent
static channels agree that the inherited exhaust target byte is never produced.

### VQ37 declares VVEL through its own DID tables

The same publication tables carry the VVEL addition positively: JK78C declares
six named VVEL identifiers and one unnamed command slot that JK08A does not
declare, and the bank-labelled pairs fix the ROM's own bank ordering. The
records, the slot, and the ordering are in `../defs/JK78C.can.md`. The target
computation, the mode machine that selects one of fifteen target pairs, the
sensor bindings, and the stored scales are in `../defs/JK78C.vvel.md`; vendor
table names remain unassigned.

JK78C carries 691 interpolation-addressed tables against 436 in JK08A.
Eighty-four of them, in `0x00017600..0x0001B800`, reproduce the exact contents
of tables in `0x0000B000..0x00017600`, 73 of those with non-constant content,
so the image holds a second copy of part of its calibration set. The census
covers interpolation addressing only.

The canonical exhaust-CVTC surfaces `0x0B2FA`, `0x0B33A`, and `0x0AB4A` are
therefore an architectural removal on VQ37 rather than relocated addresses.
All three are read only by the exhaust-target calculator. Interpolation
contract `0x261B8` is the only canonical contract with no VQ37 library
homolog, and it serves only the cam-target calculators.

The three canonical intake-CVTC source slots `0x0AB5A`, `0x0B43A`, and
`0x0B53A` are explicit no-op dispatcher slots in the reviewed VQ37 topology.
VQ37 retains intake camshaft timing control together with its P0011 and P0021
monitors and its live intake target byte, and computes that target without the
canonical surface pair. The canonical fuel-cut table `0x0934E` also remains an
unsupported source row on VQ37, but its tuning role is covered by the
target-native cap and curve maps.

No unsupported row may be replaced with a similar address or graph neighbor.
Promotion requires exact target storage, geometry, consumer path, and stock
image binding.
